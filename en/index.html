Ishigaki & Motobu Wagyu · All-You-Can-Eat

(A Night of Wagyu)

In the heart of Okinawa City, savor Okinawa's celebrated Ishigaki and Motobu wagyu — all-you-can-eat, cooked over smokeless roasters that keep the air and your clothes clean. Locals, international visitors and friends from the neighboring bases all feel at home.

Ishigaki, Motobu, and clean air.
(01) Wagyu

A quiet corner of Moromizato in Okinawa City. All-you-can-eat prefectural wagyu — including nationally famous Ishigaki beef and Motobu beef — served over smokeless roasters. No smoke, no lingering smell.

Tablet ordering supports English throughout. Locals, overseas guests, and friends from the neighboring bases share the table in comfort. Private rooms available; kids welcome.

The most relaxed premium yakiniku table in Okinawa.
A quiet, comfortable space.
(02) Interior

Seats fitted with smokeless roasters, private rooms for important nights, and an open floor with natural light. Airflow and layout designed specifically for yakiniku — comfort you can actually feel.

Tablet ordering in Japanese and English. Attentive service at the counter, with technology working quietly in the background. Everyone at the table — local or from far away — settles in.

(08) FAQ

How long is the all-you-can-eat course?
Typically about 90 minutes. Times may vary during peak periods.
Do you have an English menu?
Yes. The tablet ordering system supports Japanese and English throughout.
Are kids welcome?
Absolutely. High chairs and small plates are available. Private rooms are recommended for family visits.
Do you have private rooms?
Yes. Layout adjusts by group size — please share your party size when reserving.
Is there parking?
Free on-site parking with 14 spaces (4 surface + 10 underground).
Do you accept cashless payment?
All major credit cards, IC cards (Suica/PASMO), QR payments (PayPay, Rakuten Pay), and UnionPay are accepted.
